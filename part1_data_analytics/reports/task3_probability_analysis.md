# Task 3.1 Basic Probability Analysis

## Results

| Probability     | Value    |
|-----------------|---------:|
| P(Congestion)   | 0.1473 |
| P(Clear Weather)| 0.2778 |
| P(Congestion AND Clear Weather) | 0.0366 |

## Interpretation

Congestion was defined as traffic volume greater than 5,500 vehicles. The probability of congestion, P(Congestion), was calculated as 0.1473, indicating that congestion occurs in approximately 14.73% of all recorded observations.

The probability of clear weather, P(Clear Weather), was calculated as 0.2778. This indicates that weather conditions are classified as clear in approximately 27.78% of observations.

The probability of both congestion and clear weather occurring simultaneously, P(Congestion AND Clear Weather), was calculated as 0.0366. This indicates that only 3.66% of all observations experienced both congestion and clear weather at the same time.

These results suggest that while clear weather is relatively common, severe congestion occurs much less frequently. The low probability of congestion occurring under clear weather conditions indicates that traffic congestion may be influenced by factors beyond weather alone, such as commuting patterns, time of day, holidays, and road conditions.
