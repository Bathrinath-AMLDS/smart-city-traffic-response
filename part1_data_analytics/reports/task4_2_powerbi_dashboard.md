# Task 4.2 – Power BI Dashboard Analysis

## Objective

A Power BI Traffic Intelligence Dashboard was developed to analyse traffic trends, congestion patterns and weather impacts using the Metro Interstate Traffic Volume dataset.

---

# A. Daily Traffic Trends (2015–2017)

A line chart was created to display average daily traffic volume for the years 2015, 2016 and 2017.

### Findings

The chart shows recurring fluctuations in daily traffic volume throughout the three years. Traffic activity varies across the year due to seasonal patterns, holidays and travel behaviour.

Among the three years, 2017 generally exhibits higher traffic levels compared with 2015 and 2016, indicating continued growth in traffic demand.

### Interpretation

The visual demonstrates that traffic patterns are dynamic and influenced by temporal factors. Traffic demand generally increased over time, supporting the findings from the annual traffic trend analysis.
---

# B. Hourly Traffic Patterns (2017)

A clustered column chart was created to analyse average traffic volume by hour during 2017.

### Findings

Traffic volume is lowest during the early morning hours between midnight and 5 AM.

Traffic increases significantly from approximately 6 AM onward and remains relatively high throughout the day.

The highest traffic volume occurs at approximately 4 PM (Hour 16), where average traffic exceeds 5,800 vehicles.

### Interpretation

The chart reflects typical commuter traffic patterns with morning and evening peak periods. Congestion management strategies should focus particularly on afternoon and evening traffic peaks.

---

# C. Weather Impact Analysis

A clustered bar chart was created to compare average traffic volume across weather conditions.

### Findings

- Highest Average Traffic: Clouds
- Lowest Average Traffic: Squall
- Difference: Approximately 1,550 vehicles

### Interpretation

Cloudy weather conditions recorded the highest average traffic volume, while squall conditions recorded the lowest.

Although weather appears to influence traffic behaviour, differences between weather categories are relatively moderate. This suggests that weather alone is not the primary driver of congestion and traffic demand.

---

# D. Temperature and Traffic Volume

A scatter plot was created to examine the relationship between temperature and traffic volume.

### Findings

The visual shows a weak positive relationship between temperature and traffic volume.

Higher traffic volumes generally occur during moderate to warm temperatures, particularly between approximately 10°C and 30°C.

Several outliers are visible at extremely low temperatures and at points where traffic volumes remain low despite warmer weather.

### Interpretation

The scatter plot confirms that temperature has only a limited effect on traffic volume. While traffic tends to increase slightly as temperature increases, the relationship is weak.

This conclusion is consistent with the correlation coefficient calculated earlier:

Correlation Coefficient (r) = 0.1303

This indicates a very weak positive relationship between temperature and traffic volume.

---

# Overall Dashboard Insights

Key insights obtained from the dashboard include:

1. Traffic demand increased over time, with 2017 recording higher traffic levels than previous years.

2. Peak traffic activity occurs during afternoon commuting hours.

3. Weather conditions influence traffic patterns, although the effect is relatively limited.

4. Temperature has only a weak relationship with traffic volume.

5. Traffic congestion is influenced by multiple factors including commuting behaviour, time of day, holidays and weather conditions.

---

## Conclusion

The Power BI Traffic Intelligence Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of traffic behaviour and congestion patterns. The visualisations demonstrate how traffic varies across time, weather conditions and temperature ranges. These insights can support data-driven traffic management and smart city planning initiatives.
