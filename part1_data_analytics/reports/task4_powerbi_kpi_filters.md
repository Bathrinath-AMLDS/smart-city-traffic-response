# Task 4.3 – KPI Cards and Filters

## KPI Cards

Three KPI cards were created to provide summary information for the dashboard.

### Total Hours Analysed

The Total Hours Analysed KPI displays the total number of hourly observations available within the dataset.

Result: 48,204 observations.

### Average Traffic Volume

The Average Traffic Volume KPI displays the mean hourly traffic volume across the dataset.

Result: Approximately 3,260 vehicles.

### Average Temperature

The Average Temperature KPI displays the average recorded temperature after conversion from Kelvin to Celsius.

Result: Approximately 8.06°C.

---

## Interactive Filters

Three slicers were added to improve dashboard interactivity.

### Hour Range

Allows users to analyse traffic patterns for selected hours of the day and investigate peak and off-peak conditions.

### Weather Condition

Allows users to filter dashboard visuals according to weather categories such as Clouds, Clear, Rain and Snow.

### Traffic Category

Allows users to filter observations based on congestion level classifications:

- Low
- Medium
- High

---

## Interpretation

The KPI cards provide a concise summary of the dataset, while the slicers allow users to interactively explore traffic behaviour from multiple perspectives. Together, these features improve dashboard usability and support more effective traffic intelligence analysis.
