Task 2.2 Correlation Analysis
Correlation Between Temperature and Traffic Volume
Correlation Coefficient (r): 0.1303

Direction of Relationship
The correlation coefficient is positive, indicating that traffic volume tends to increase slightly as temperature increases.

Strength of Relationship
The correlation coefficient of 0.1303 indicates a very weak positive relationship between temperature and traffic volume. Temperature appears to have only a limited influence on traffic levels.

Why Correlation Does Not Imply Causation
A correlation between temperature and traffic volume does not necessarily mean that temperature directly causes changes in traffic volume. Other factors such as time of day, day of week, holidays, seasonal travel behaviour, weather conditions, and road incidents may also influence traffic demand. Correlation measures association between variables but does not establish a cause-and-effect relationship.
